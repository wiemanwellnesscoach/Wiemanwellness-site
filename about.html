About Wieman Wellness

Live Fully. Track Mindfully. Thrive Daily.

Our Story

We’re Jason and Delilah Wieman. After decades serving military families and coaching households through tough transitions, we saw the same truth repeat: wellness isn’t one-dimensional. People flourish when money, mind, body, faith, relationships, and purpose are aligned.

Wieman Wellness grew out of that conviction. We combine compassionate financial coaching, evidence-informed mental wellness supports, and faith-centered practices to help you build rhythms of peace and growth.

What we value

  • Compassion over criticism
  • Clarity over confusion
  • Consistency over quick fixes
  • Stewardship & service

Dr. Jason Wieman

Retired Army physician with 36 years of service, Jason now dedicates his work to supporting the mental health of service members and their families. He brings a steady, compassionate presence and a whole-person approach to care.

Delilah Wieman

Financial coach and educator helping people move from stress to stability. Delilah equips clients with practical tools, simple systems, and encouragement to build confidence with money and daily habits.
