About Jason & Delilah

We’re Jason and Delilah Wieman — the heart and hands behind Wieman Wellness. Rooted in resilience, service, and a shared calling to help others navigate life’s transitions, Wieman Wellness is our way of offering support that’s both practical and deeply personal.

Dr. Jason Wieman is a retired Army physician who now supports the mental health of service members and their families. Delilah Wieman helps individuals and families bring clarity, confidence, and calm to their relationship with money — transforming stress into stability and purpose.

Together, we guide others through six pillars of wellness — mental, physical, financial, relational, vocational, and spiritual — so they can live fully, track mindfully, and thrive daily.
